# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-17

First published release for GNOME Shell 50.

### Included

- Browse JPG, JPEG, and PNG memes from recursively scanned local folders.
- Search local meme filenames from the GNOME top bar.
- Optionally search Imgflip or GIPHY online.
- Copy local and online memes to the clipboard.
- Save online results to `~/Pictures/Memes`.
- Configure meme folders and online search settings in Preferences.
- Keep the selector panel width stable, reserve scrollbar space, and reveal
  long meme names by wrapping them on hover.
